SDN (Software-Defined Networking) – tor dolandyryşyny programma üsti bilen merkezleşdirmek. Tor enjamlary (switç, router) merkezi kontroller tarapyndan dolandyrylýar.

Arhitektura:

  • Application Layer: Tor programmalary.
  • Control Layer: Merkezi kontroller (SDN Controller).
  • Infrastructure Layer: Fiziki switçler we routerler.

Protokollar: OpenFlow, NETCONF, RESTCONF.
